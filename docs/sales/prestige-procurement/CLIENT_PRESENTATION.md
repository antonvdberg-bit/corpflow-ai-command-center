## Hook

You should own a site that looks like Prestige, that your team can update on Monday morning, and that still works if CorpFlowAI is not in the chat.

That is the job. Not a rented page on our platform. Not a monthly “we’ll handle the website” fee.

## Buyer problem

Most B2B sites fail in one of three ways:

1. They look generic, so a serious buyer does not trust the company.
2. They look good, but only the agency can change a service page.
3. Hosting, DNS, and backups sit in the agency’s account, so leaving is painful.

Prestige asked for the opposite: a **custom-designed** site, a **full self-management suite**, and **independent hosting** Prestige can select and pay for.

## CorpFlowAI mechanism

We design and build once. We put WordPress on **your** hosting. We train your editor. We hand over backups and admin. We stay for a 30-day defect warranty. Then the site is yours.

We are not proposing a CorpFlowAI-hosted marketing tenant. Those are our internal test surfaces. Your live site should be on your domain and your host.

## Proof

What we can stand behind today:

- A written 12-phase plan with owners (us / you / your host).
- An independence test: your editor publishes a change; you can download a backup; hosting is in your name.
- Commercial record in ERPNext (quotation → project → milestone invoices) so payment and delivery stay aligned.
- Honest exclusions: no ranking guarantee, no sales guarantee, no hidden retainer.

What we do **not** yet have: a Prestige-specific visual mock. That is Phase 3, after mobilisation — not something we fake in this meeting.

## Depth

### What you get (base)

- About eight pages, custom look on phone and desktop.
- You edit pages, services, photos, and the enquiry form without calling us.
- Enquiry emails go to **your** mailbox.
- Backups you can see. A restore we rehearse once before handover.
- Training (recorded) plus a short editor guide.

### What you pay us vs what you pay others

| You pay CorpFlowAI (one-off) | You pay directly |
|------------------------------|------------------|
| Design, build, training, handover, 30-day warranty | Hosting (monthly) |
| Recommended **MUR 285,000** | Domain (you already own, or you register) |
| | Any stock photos or extra licences we agree first |

No CorpFlowAI monthly website fee in this offer.

### How long

**8–12 weeks** after the first payment clears and we have your brand files plus the facts for each page. We will not put a calendar date on a slide until you lock scope.

If copy is late, the end date moves. That is the only honest rule.

### How you pay (not 50/50)

| When | Share | What you have in hand |
|------|------:|------------------------|
| Start | 20% | We begin discovery |
| You accept design | 20% | Locked look and feel |
| Staging is real | 25% | Pages + editor access on staging |
| You say “go live” | 20% | QA’d site ready for your domain |
| You accept handover | 15% | Training done, site yours |

You never pay the whole fee before you have seen the work. We never build the whole site on a handshake.

### What we will not do in this price

- Run your hosting forever.
- Guarantee Google or more tenders.
- Build a shop / stock system / client login.
- Unlimited design changes.
- Keep an invisible CorpFlowAI copy of the site that we control.

## Self-management in practice

After training, a Prestige editor should be able to:

1. Change Home headline and a service paragraph.
2. Add a PDF brochure.
3. See new enquiries in your email.
4. Invite a second editor without giving them full admin.
5. Download a backup.

If we cannot demonstrate that, we have not finished.

## Governance

- One named approver on your side.
- Two structured revision rounds (you send one written list; we apply it).
- Staging first; your domain only after you write “proceed to launch.”
- We remove our admin at the end of warranty.

## Handover

You receive: the live site on your host, editor guide, recording, export, and credentials via a password manager — not in a WhatsApp screenshot.

## Commercial structure (say this once, slowly)

- **Currency:** MUR.
- **Fee:** MUR 285,000 one-off (Anton may confirm or choose Option B at MUR 165,000 if you want a branded theme instead of a full custom UI).
- **Hosting:** you choose, you pay.
- **Validity:** 14 days once the official quotation is issued from ERPNext.

## Next action (single CTA)

**Confirm the page list and choose Option A or B.** Then Anton issues the official MUR quotation. Payment instructions follow after you accept in writing. We do not start build on a verbal yes.

Lower-friction path: if the number is the blocker, say so now and we switch to Option B in this meeting — we do not invent a fourth architecture on the whiteboard.
