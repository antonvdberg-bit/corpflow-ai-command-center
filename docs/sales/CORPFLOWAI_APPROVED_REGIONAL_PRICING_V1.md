## Scope boundaries

### Lead Rescue

Standard setup covers one source for one business, up to 500 enquiry records per month:

- map the enquiry path;
- configure agreed capture and alerts;
- test the workflow with the client and obtain acceptance;
- automated health checks where installed;
- working-day internal triage;
- weekly review;
- monthly recap;
- up to 60 minutes of agreed improvements in monthly care.

The client team replies to prospects and closes sales. Verify the installation cadence before
promising it. No unverified WhatsApp API capability is promised.

### Website Rescue

Standard setup covers one premium landing page or similarly bounded repair, mobile and
enquiry-path checks, and two consolidated review rounds. Monthly care covers one supported
site of up to five pages, weekly health checks, monthly enquiry and technical-search checks,
a report, and up to 60 minutes of minor improvements.

Larger rebuilds, e-commerce, extra functions, paid media, third-party licences, hosting
changes and major new work receive a separate quote.

### Business automation

Automation is scoped as fixed deliverables with agreed testing, milestones and handover.
Anton oversight and acceptance remain part of the commercial path. Separate software costs,
access, dependencies and timing are confirmed in writing. Confirm delivery timing individually;
never promise a date by implication.

## Payment and timing

We confirm fit, scope, delivery timing, applicable taxes, the total price and payment
instructions in writing before commitment. No payment is taken on the public pricing page.
Monthly care is a separate purchase and starts only after setup acceptance and explicit opt-in.
Payment eligibility, taxes, renewal and refund terms remain subject to confirmation before sale.

## Separate offers

The Mauritius Lead Rescue / Enquiry Recovery Sprint remains a distinct scoped offer at its
accepted price. These standard packages do not reprice or replace that sprint. Property and
premium funnels retain their separately scoped offers.

This guide records the approved standard-package atom for the `/pricing` validation path.
It does not claim universal legal compliance, guaranteed rankings, guaranteed revenue or
automatic payment availability.
