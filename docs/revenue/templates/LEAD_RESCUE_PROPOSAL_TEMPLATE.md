## 2. Problem and desired outcome

**Problem today:** `<e.g. enquiries arrive but responses are delayed or missed>`  

**Desired outcome:** Every new enquiry on the agreed source is visible quickly; owner has a clear daily summary and a simple follow-up process during the pilot window.

## 3. Included scope

- Identify / confirm **one** primary leaky enquiry source.
- Connect that source into the agreed Lead Rescue pilot workflow.
- **48-hour** setup window after payment clears (or after approved exception start date).
- **7-day** monitoring window with daily summary to agreed channels.
- Operator alert path for the connected source (operator-side).
- Documented lead stages, escalation rules, and handover note at end of setup.
- End-of-pilot recap with activity numbers (no revenue guarantees).

## 4. Exclusions

- No second lead source during the pilot setup window (unless separately quoted).
- No WhatsApp / email / SMS **automated runtime** without separate messaging-runtime authorisation.
- No full CRM migration or historical database import.
- No website redesign / DNS / production deploy as part of this proposal.
- No pricing, medical, legal, or lead-volume / revenue guarantees.
- No collection of passwords, OTPs, card data, or government IDs in chat.

## 5. Assumptions

- Client names one working WhatsApp and one working email that the owner monitors.
- Client can provide access or forwarding needed for the single agreed source.
- Client replies to customers; CorpFlow surfaces and follows process — it does not pretend to be the business without agreement.

## 6. Client responsibilities

- Provide the named leaky source and keep it available during the pilot.
- Name one approver for feedback within the agreed review cadence.
- Escalate access / owner-availability blockers within one business day.
- Confirm acceptance of exclusions in writing.

## 7. Implementation / pilot approach

1. Kick-off and source confirmation.  
2. Setup within 48 hours of cleared payment (or exception start).  
3. Marked test enquiry verification.  
4. Daily summaries for 7 days.  
5. Recap and optional monthly monitoring quote.
