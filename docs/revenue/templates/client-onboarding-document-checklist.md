# Client onboarding document checklist

**Use:** Collect documents before or during sprint. Store in **ERPNext** (system of record) — not ad-hoc folders.

---

## Identity & contract

- [ ] Legal business name and BRN (if applicable)
- [ ] Primary contact name, email, phone
- [ ] Billing contact (if different)
- [ ] Signed quote or email approval with scope summary
- [ ] Deposit verification record (date, amount, reference)

## Access (offer-specific)

### AI Lead Rescue Sprint

- [ ] Lead source access or forwarding instructions
- [ ] Alert recipient name and phone/email
- [ ] Existing enquiry examples (redact customer PII)

### Premium Landing Page Rescue

- [ ] Logo files (vector preferred)
- [ ] Brand colours / font preferences
- [ ] Approved photography or stock direction
- [ ] Offer copy in plain language
- [ ] Named production approver

### Customer Recovery Sprint

- [ ] Sample complaints or reviews (redacted)
- [ ] Refund / complaint policy boundaries
- [ ] Review platform access or forwarding
- [ ] Legal escalation contact if needed

## Delivery & release

- [ ] Preview feedback captured (date + decision)
- [ ] Production release approval (written)
- [ ] Balance payment verification (if applicable)
- [ ] Handover note filed in project / WBS

## Maintenance (if sold)

- [ ] Maintenance acceptance email
- [ ] Monthly invoice contact

---

**Rule:** Missing items block only the affected milestone — deposit-verified clock starts at clearance, not at document perfection. Chase blockers daily until received.
