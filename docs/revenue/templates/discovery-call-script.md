# Discovery call script (15 minutes)

**Use:** Qualify fit before quote or deposit. **No slides. No demo.** The offer page is the deck.

---

## Opening (1 min)

```text
Hi {first name}, thanks for the time — I'll keep this to about 15 minutes.

I help businesses stop losing leads, customers, and reputation when digital
operations are slow or invisible. This call is to see if one of our rapid
delivery sprints fits — not to pitch generic AI or a CRM rebuild.

I'll ask how enquiries or customers reach you today. At the end we'll either
agree a clear next step or I'll say honestly if we're not the right fit.

Sound okay?
```

Wait for verbal yes.

---

## Pain (4 min)

| # | Question | Listen for |
| - | -------- | ---------- |
| 1 | What led you to look at this **now**? | Trigger event — missed deal, review spike, weak site |
| 2 | Where do {enquiries / customers / complaints} arrive today? | Channels verbatim |
| 3 | Where does it feel **most leaky**? | Specific channel or handoff failure |
| 4 | Can you name a **recent example** in the last 30 days? | Real pain; if none, soft close |

---

## Fit (4 min)

| # | Question | Listen for |
| - | -------- | ---------- |
| 5 | Who handles this day to day — you or someone on the team? | Named owner for alerts/responses |
| 6 | Rough volume per week? | Sizing (5/week fine; 200/week may need custom quote) |
| 7 | What does **success in 72 hours** look like for you? | Buyer language for quote scope |

---

## Offer routing (2 min)

| Signal | Offer |
| ------ | ----- |
| Enquiries lost across channels | AI Lead Rescue Sprint — `/offers/ai-lead-rescue` |
| Site weak + enquiry path broken | Premium Landing Page Rescue — `/offers/premium-landing-page-rescue` |
| Reviews / complaints backlog | Customer Recovery Sprint — `/offers/customer-reputation-recovery` |

State **starting price in MUR**, **50% deposit**, and **no revenue guarantees**.

---

## Close (4 min)

**Fit — next step:**

```text
Based on what you shared, {offer name} looks like the right sprint.

Next step: I'll send a short written quote with scope, deposit, and timeline.
Work starts only after manual bank verification of cleared funds.

Can I send that to {email} within {today / tomorrow}?
```

**Not fit — soft close:**

```text
I don't think we're the right sprint for you right now because {reason}.
Happy to revisit if {trigger} changes.
```

---

## Capture within 1 hour

Log in ERPNext (when configured) or private prospect list:

- Business name, contact, channels, pain quote (verbatim)
- Offer selected, starting price discussed
- Next step + date promised
