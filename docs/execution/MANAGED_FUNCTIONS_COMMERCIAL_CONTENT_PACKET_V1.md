## 1. Company-level positioning

### Corporate proposition

**Enterprise-grade business functions, operated for SMEs.**

### Plain-language proposition

**Get the business function you need, without building the department around it.**

### Supporting commercial line

**Hire the function, not the software.**

### Internal doctrine

**Capability is abundant. Competence is managed.**

### Operating reassurance

**Technology changes. Your business function keeps working.**

### What CorpFlowAI is

CorpFlowAI operates defined business functions for SMEs using the appropriate mix of people, AI, software, automation, client systems and governed authority. The customer buys reliable performance of the function, not a new technology stack.

### What CorpFlowAI is not

CorpFlowAI is not positioned as:
- a generic AI-agent company;
- a chatbot reseller;
- a generic automation consultancy;
- a CRM replacement;
- a social-media posting agency;
- a bespoke ERP vendor;
- a generic dashboard builder.

## 2. Public function architecture

### Managed Growth

**Get customers. Develop them. Keep them. Grow them.**

Operating chain:
**Find → Attract → Capture → Nurture → Convert → Retain → Expand → Recover**

Representative functions:
- prospect and market identification;
- content and visibility;
- social and campaign operations;
- paid advertising management where separately approved/budgeted;
- enquiry capture;
- lead nurturing;
- appointment and quote follow-up;
- customer retention;
- customer expansion / cross-sell / upsell opportunity identification;
- dormant customer reactivation;
- enquiry and revenue recovery.

### Managed Finance Operations

**Keep financial administration moving, visible and controlled.**

Representative functions:
- debtor follow-up;
- creditor administration;
- reconciliation preparation;
- payment preparation;
- cash / finance reporting;
- finance exception management.

### Managed Business Operations

**Keep recurring operating work from falling between people and systems.**

Representative functions:
- scheduling administration;
- document administration;
- stock and reorder monitoring;
- procurement administration;
- expiry/waste monitoring;
- recurring operational follow-up.

### Managed Management Intelligence

**Give owners and managers a usable operating picture without assembling it manually.**

Representative functions:
- daily/weekly business pulse;
- exception monitoring;
- pipeline visibility;
- management reporting;
- performance summaries;
- decision-support packs.

## 3. First commercial wedge

### Managed Growth first

CorpFlowAI should lead with Managed Growth because revenue leakage is easier for an SME buyer to understand than a general outsourcing proposition.

The first diagnostic offer is the **Growth Leakage Review**.

Primary question:
> Where is the business failing to find, convert, retain, expand or recover revenue?

The review must not assume a predetermined software implementation. Its purpose is to identify the smallest managed function that fixes the largest credible leak.

## 7. SEO and AI-discoverability posture

Defined in:

`docs/marketing/managed-functions/SEO_AI_DISCOVERABILITY_V1.md`

Core principles:
- useful, original operating content rather than generic AI commentary;
- clear page hierarchy and internal linking;
- crawlable, indexable HTML;
- accurate titles, descriptions and canonicals;
- appropriate Article / Organization / Service structured data where valid;
- visible authorship and publish/update dates;
- text support/transcripts for video;
- allow OAI-SearchBot unless a later explicit policy says otherwise;
- retain conventional SEO discipline; do not build speculative GEO/AEO infrastructure;
- use free/native measurement first (Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, existing analytics where configured).

Current external guidance checked 2026-09-10:
- OpenAI says public sites can appear in ChatGPT search and recommends not blocking OAI-SearchBot for summaries/snippets/citations.
- Microsoft Bing Webmaster Tools now exposes AI Performance reporting for citations across Copilot and AI-generated Bing experiences.
- Google Search Central's 2026 guidance continues to emphasize normal SEO foundations and non-commodity content for generative search experiences.

These are implementation requirements, not marketing claims.

## 9. Content reuse system

One substantive commercial idea should yield multiple assets without turning into content sludge.

Minimum reuse pattern:
- flagship article;
- landing-page section;
- 30–60 second video;
- 2–4 short clips or frames;
- social post;
- FAQ/objection answer;
- optional one-page graphic;
- future newsletter/email item.

The source idea stays constant. The channel treatment changes.
