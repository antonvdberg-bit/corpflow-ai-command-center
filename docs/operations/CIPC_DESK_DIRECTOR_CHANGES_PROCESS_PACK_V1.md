## Official process backbone (confirmed from CIPC)

**OFFICIAL — electronic channel.** Director changes are done electronically. The enhanced director-amendments process launched **04 December 2023**. The current e-Service is **CoR39** (Director Amendments).

**OFFICIAL — standard electronic sequence (2023/2024 step-by-step PDFs):**

1. Customer login / registration (valid CIPC customer code).
2. Open the director-amendments / CoR39 e-Service for the enterprise.
3. Review current directors on the CIPC record.
4. Capture the change: appoint, resign, update particulars, or (where the system offers it) another status.
5. Attach documents **if required**, then continue.
6. Application moves to submitted / OTP status.
7. The **filer** and the **directors being appointed or resigned** receive a Proposed Changes email, review the attached document, and complete identification + SMS OTP + email OTP, then agree and submit.
8. After OTPs are complete, finalise the application.
9. Active directors receive an email with the CoR39 attached.

**OFFICIAL — OTP dependency.** Resignation requires an OTP from the **resigning director**. Appointment requires OTP completion by the **filer and the director being appointed**. Particulars updates sit on the same electronic family.

**OFFICIAL — document optionality on the electronic path (2023/2024 PDFs):**

| Status / action | Documents on the electronic path |
|-----------------|----------------------------------|
| Update director details | Optional |
| Appoint a director | Optional |
| Resign a director | Optional |
| Remove | Mandatory |
| Deceased | Mandatory |
| Term expired | Mandatory |

**OFFICIAL — email / alternative lodgement path (CIPC alternative-process page + older CoR39 guide):** supporting documents may be emailed to `eServicesCOR39@cipc.co.za` (electronic CoR39) or `manualcor39@cipc.co.za`. The older guide requires the **tracking number first** in the email subject or the email is automatically rejected.

**OFFICIAL — identity proof on the email path.** Passport copy is accepted as identity proof **only for non-residents of South Africa**. South African residents must lodge a green bar-coded / smart ID copy.

**OFFICIAL — company-law / MOI facts named by CIPC (alternative-process page):**

- A private company may not have **less than one director**.
- A **customised MOI** may set eligibility and term of office.
- On a **standard MOI**, term of office is **indefinite** and there is no restriction on the number of directors.
- New directors may be elected by the board when there is a vacancy or the company wishes to add directors, **unless the MOI requires shareholder approval**.
- Vacancies arise if a director resigns or dies; ceases to hold the office that entitles an ex officio director; becomes incapacitated or disqualified; or is removed.
- Removal routes named by CIPC: ordinary shareholder resolution (with notice and a reasonable opportunity for the director to present); board resolution for ineligibility / disqualification / incapacity / neglect under section 69; or court order confirming board removal.

**OFFICIAL — current troubleshooting (Notice 2 of 2024):**

- Customer-profile identification change from passport to ID: log a ticket on https://enquiries.cipc.co.za (Department — Login Challenges) with customer code, passport number, and ID number, plus a certified copy of the ID. Back office updates the profile.
- South African director domiciled overseas: capture the overseas address, select any South African province to finalise, then log an enquiry (Companies and CCs → Company Amendments → Director Amendments EService (CoR39)) with enterprise number, director ID, and province/state so back office can correct the record.
- ID issue-date error when appointing: do **not** type the date; use the calendar control. The date must match Home Affairs records.
- International cellphone: do **not** add a `+`. Example given by CIPC: Zimbabwe number captured as `263770000000`.

**OFFICIAL — sole-director simultaneous resignation + appointment (practice note 2 of 2021, as cited by CIPC):** if a company has a sole director and the board effects resignation **together with** appointment of a new director, the resolution must be **co-signed by both directors**.

**PRACTICAL — Desk v1 standard scenarios (this pack; pending Sarah):**

- **Appointment** — standard review path.
- **Resignation** — standard review path, gated on resigning-director OTP / contactability.
- **Change to director particulars / contact details** — standard review path where the current electronic process supports it.
- **Retirement / term expiry** — present as **conditional**. Official electronic guides group Term Expired with the resignation process shape but make documents **mandatory**. Treat as specialist until Sarah confirms a safe standard route.
- **Death / removal** — **not** v1 standard. Specialist-review / exception only. Official electronic guides make documents **mandatory**. Removal also carries Companies Act process (notice, presentation, possible court confirmation) that Desk must not collapse into a routine resignation.

**SARAH CONFIRM — entity scope:** whether v1 standard Director Changes covers **private companies only**, or also **close-corporation member changes**, NPCs, or other entity types.

# Layer 1 — Customer explanation

*Audience: client or referring professional. Plain English. Not legal advice.*

### What a director change is

**OFFICIAL / plain-English paraphrase:** A director change is a filing with CIPC that updates the company’s official director record. Typical updates are appointing a director, recording a resignation, or correcting a director’s particulars. CIPC now handles these electronically through the CoR39 director-amendments service. It is **not** an Annual Return, **not** a SARS filing, and **not** a Beneficial Ownership filing (those may still be needed separately if ownership or control also changed).

### What the CIPC Desk Director Changes service covers

**PRACTICAL (provisional v1 — standard scenarios only):**

- Helping the client understand what CIPC’s electronic director-amendments process requires for a **standard appointment, resignation, or particulars update**.
- Collecting the facts and supporting items needed for a complete filing attempt.
- Checking whether the instructing party appears authorised, whether the company will still have at least one director, and whether OTP / contact details look usable.
- Preparing and submitting a **standard** electronic CoR39 matter **only after** authority and required information are complete (**SARAH CONFIRM** whether a signed engagement/mandate is a hard gate, as on Annual Returns).
- Capturing proof (CoR39 / confirmation email) and telling the client what was filed.
- Escalating to specialist review when the matter is outside a standard path.

**What the service does *not* automatically include (provisional v1 exclusions):**

- Death of a director, forced removal, court-ordered removal, or contested / disputed director changes.
- Term-expiry / retirement filings until Sarah confirms they are a safe standard route.
- Changing a customer-profile identification from passport to ID (CIPC back-office enquiry path).
- Repairing Home Affairs mismatches, overseas-address province workarounds, or CIPC system defects.
- Beneficial Ownership amendments, share transfers, MOI changes, or restorations discovered during intake.
- Guaranteeing that CIPC will accept, process, or complete any filing, or that every director will complete OTP.
- Acting as CIPC or giving formal legal opinions.
- Any Desk service-fee or pricing figures until the commercial pricing model is approved.

### What the client must provide

**PRACTICAL — typical inputs (conditional; not every item for every matter):**

- Company registration number and registered name.
- Confirmation of entity type and whether the MOI is standard or customised.
- Which scenario applies: appointment, resignation, particulars update, or (if raised) term expiry / death / removal.
- Current director list as the client believes it stands, plus what CIPC currently shows if known.
- Affected director full names, identity/passport details, residential / postal address, email, and cellphone (OTP depends on working contact details).
- For appointment: signed consent to act, and any resolution / minutes the company already has.
- For resignation: signed resignation letter, and confirmation the resigning director can receive SMS and email OTPs.
- Instructing-person details and relationship to the company.
- Mandate / power of attorney if a third party will file.
- **SARAH CONFIRM:** signed Desk engagement/mandate before filing; customer-code path (client’s own code vs authorised practitioner code).

### What CIPC Desk cannot guarantee

**PRACTICAL:**

- CIPC processing time or same-day completion.
- That every director will receive or complete OTP.
- That a filing will succeed on first attempt.
- That a contested removal or death update will be accepted as a routine resignation.
- That BO, Annual Return, or MOI issues discovered during intake will be resolved as part of this service.

Any timing estimate must be qualified as subject to CIPC processing times, system availability, OTP completion, submission quality, additional-information requests, and manual review.

### When specialist review may be required

**PRACTICAL / provisional specialist triggers:**

- Death, removal, court order, or any contested / disputed change.
- Term expiry / retirement until Sarah confirms a standard route.
- Sole-director resignation without a simultaneous appointment (company would drop below one director).
- Sole-director simultaneous resignation + appointment (practice note 2 of 2021 — specialist check).
- Customised MOI eligibility, shareholder-approval, or fixed-term clauses.
- Foreign director / foreigner-assurance path.
- Identity/passport vs Home Affairs mismatch; customer-profile ID change.
- Existing CIPC record does not match the client’s understanding.
- Unreachable director who cannot complete OTP.
- Suspected false or unverifiable information.
