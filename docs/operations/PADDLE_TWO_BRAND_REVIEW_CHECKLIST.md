# Paddle two-brand onboarding review checklist

**Status:** Review preparation only; no Paddle submission, checkout, payment
catalogue, or acceptance is claimed.

**Brands and environments**

- CorpFlowAI Ltd / CorpFlowAI pricing is the approved permanent regional pricing
  surface on the core routes.
- Business Admin Desk is operated by CorpFlowAI Ltd and has a separate
  review-only pricing view. Its final numerical rates are not approved.
- CorpFlowAI-hosted review routes are `corpflow_test`. The Business Admin Desk
  public host remains `client_production` and must not inherit this review
  content.

## Pricing evidence

CorpFlowAI’s accepted selected-market prices are documented in
`docs/sales/CORPFLOWAI_APPROVED_REGIONAL_PRICING_V1.md`. Rates are selected
market prices, not foreign-exchange calculations. Setup and optional monthly
care are separate, and care requires explicit opt-in after setup acceptance.

The customer buys a complete package: CorpFlowAI builds or configures the
agreed system, includes the stated AI processing and human verification,
tests it against the acceptance outcome, and hands it over under client
control. Small client login or logging environments are package-specific
exceptions. Any ongoing care requires separately agreed authorised access.
Internal bill of materials, private candidate rates, human cost data, and
capacity data are not review evidence.

Business Admin Desk is intentionally quote-only in this review surface:
`QUOTED IN ZAR / pilot rates under review`. A written quote must itemise the
service fee, one-off setup, statutory fee, tax, and total before commitment.
Statutory fees are separate; complex or cleanup work is separately quoted.
There are no automatic portfolio discounts, invented retainers, outcome
guarantees, or authority-turnaround guarantees.

## Policy and approval boundaries

- Historical accepted quotations retain their agreed terms; the retired
  MUR 85,000 sprint is not an active separate offer.
- No page claims that bank routes, Paddle, checkout, or payment activation are
  currently live.
- No new cancellation, refund, tax, retention, legal-compliance, ranking,
  revenue, WhatsApp API, or delivery-time promise is silently binding.
- Any incomplete legal language belongs in a clearly labelled draft-for-review
  section, not in the operative policy pages.
- A noindex directive is review hygiene, not access control. No private
  commercial values or personal records may be placed on an anonymous route.

## Final gates before any submission

1. Confirm final commercial approval for every package and brand.
2. Obtain legal review of the operative policy text and buyer rights.
3. Verify the real contact channels and legal/company identity evidence.
4. Verify HTTPS and the exact domains intended for review.
5. Confirm Paddle product-category eligibility from the complete package
   description and configured-system fulfilment evidence.
6. Publish only after the required approval; record deployed commit and URL
   evidence separately from this preparation document.
7. Treat Paddle’s actual acceptance as a separate result. Pricing pages,
   screenshots, and a custom price document do not equal acceptance.
