# Business Admin Desk — Public Marketing Values

Status: Firm marketing standard
Owner: CorpFlowAI / Business Admin Desk

## Value 1 — Own our message; do not market through another organisation's brand

Business Admin Desk stands on its own as a company-administration service.

Public-facing Business Admin Desk marketing must not use another regulator's or organisation's brand name, acronym, logo, website, interface, document imagery, or identifiable branding as a marketing device.

This applies to:
- landing pages and public service pages;
- marketing videos, captions and subtitles;
- advertisements and social content;
- public partner/acquisition pages;
- brochures, presentations and promotional PDFs;
- public email campaigns and marketing collateral;
- public metadata, Open Graph copy and imagery.

Use neutral customer language such as:
- company administration;
- company requirements;
- statutory requirements;
- regulatory requirements;
- filing requirements;
- company records;
- company information;
- regulatory processes.

This is a marketing-layer rule, not an instruction to corrupt operational or statutory accuracy. Internal technical identifiers may remain stable. Where a regulator or other third party must genuinely be identified in a client instruction, statutory form, filing workflow, evidence record, legal/compliance disclosure, or transaction-specific communication, use the accurate name only to the extent required.

### Customer proposition

**You run your business. We help with the administration.**

Business Admin Desk should reduce administrative complexity rather than require prospects to learn the regulator ecosystem before engaging us.

### Independence

Where an independence statement is needed publicly, prefer:

**Independent company-administration support. Not a government or regulatory service.**

Do not imply government, regulator, legal-firm, or official endorsement.

## Enforcement

New public-facing Business Admin Desk marketing should be reviewed against Value 1 before release. Existing public marketing should be migrated toward this standard as part of the current Business Admin Desk brand/marketing cleanup.
