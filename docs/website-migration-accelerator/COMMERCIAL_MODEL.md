# WMA Commercial Model

Approved under issue #1284 on 2026-09-14.

## 1. Free Prospect Snapshot
Purpose: open a commercial conversation with a small evidence-backed teaser.

Deliver:
- three material findings
- one lead-path observation
- one recommendation

Do not provide the full migration inventory, implementation plan, or unlimited free consulting.

## 2. Website Migration Diagnostic
Approved price band: **MUR 7,500-12,500**.

Expected output:
- public site inventory
- page map
- content/migration decisions
- lead-path audit
- technical/SEO first pass
- visual/mobile review when rendered evidence is available
- target sitemap
- client opportunity summary
- fixed-scope implementation recommendation

The diagnostic fee may be credited toward a Website Rescue engagement when commercially appropriate.

## 3. Website Rescue
Reference starting point: **MUR 45,000+**, scope dependent.

Use where evidence supports targeted migration, consolidation, conversion, responsive, route/redirect, or content work.

## 4. Product A expansion
Only where evidence supports it:
- AI Lead Rescue
- ElevenLabs receptionist
- ERPNext Lead / Opportunity capture
- Langfuse later for AI tracing; outside WMA implementation.

## Positioning
Do not sell “a better website.” Sell a cleaner route from buyer intent to an owned business outcome.

## Strong diagnostic candidate
Typical indicators:
- commercially important site
- multiple lead/contact paths
- legacy routes/content
- meaningful trust/SEO content to preserve
- unclear enquiry ownership
- planned migration/rebuild

## Weak candidate
- no material website problem
- issue is traffic rather than conversion
- assessment requires private/login access
- prospect is best served by a commodity low-cost builder

## Guardrail
No autonomous prospect outreach. Any client-facing send requires Anton approval.
