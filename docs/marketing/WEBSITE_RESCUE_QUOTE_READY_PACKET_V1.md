## 1. Exact problem solved

Many small businesses keep a homepage that looks outdated, fails on mobile, or never asks for the enquiry. Buyers bounce before they understand the offer. The owner knows the site “isn’t working” but cannot write a web brief.

**Website Rescue (public SKU: Premium Landing Page Rescue) fixes:** a credible, mobile-ready landing (or bounded brochure) with one clear buyer-action CTA and a tested enquiry path — delivered on a managed preview path before production release. It is a **managed landing rescue**, not a months-long custom platform programme, SEO campaign, or revenue guarantee.

---

## 2. Target customer

**Strong fit:**

- Mauritius (or similar) owner-operator whose site under-represents the business.
- Pain is **credibility + enquiry capture**, not “build me a custom app.”
- Can approve a MUR deposit without procurement theatre.
- Willing to supply logo, offer summary, and a named approver (or accept guided options).

**Not in scope for standard quotes:** enterprise multi-site programmes, e-commerce platforms, member portals, guaranteed ranking/traffic, or buyers who need Lead Rescue **instead of** a site (route those to Lead Rescue).
