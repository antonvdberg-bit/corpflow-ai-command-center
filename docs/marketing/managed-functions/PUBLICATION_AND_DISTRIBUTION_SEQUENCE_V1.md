# Managed Functions — Publication and Distribution Sequence v1

**Status:** Commercial execution plan. Docs only; no external send, ad spend or production publication authorized.

## Objective

Turn the managed-business-function thesis into a small, coherent public body of work that can begin building search, AI discoverability and buyer understanding before international payment rails are finalized.

## Phase 0 — before any publication

Required:
- Anton approves the public positioning;
- #1252/#1274 governance conflict is explicitly resolved for this bounded sprint;
- implementation preview exists;
- metadata/crawler/internal-link checks pass;
- Marketing Quality Gate ≥12/14;
- no new paid tool is introduced.

## Phase 1 — category establishment

Publish together or in a tight sequence:

1. **Managed Growth pillar page**
2. **What Is a Managed Business Function?**
3. **Managed Growth for SMEs**

Purpose:
Teach both buyers and search/AI systems the company category and first commercial family.

Distribution:
- CorpFlowAI website / Insights;
- founder/company LinkedIn where available;
- existing CorpFlowAI social channels where already active;
- no paid amplification required.

## Phase 2 — commercial problem content

Publish:

4. **Lead Generation Is Not Enough**
5. **How Much Revenue Is Your Business Losing Through Poor Follow-Up?**
6. Video: **Get the Opportunity**
7. Video: **Don't Waste the Opportunity**

Purpose:
Move from abstract category to a concrete, commercially urgent problem.

Every attention asset points to:
- the relevant article;
- Managed Growth;
- or the Growth Leakage Review.

## Phase 3 — first diagnostic demand

Activate the **Growth Leakage Review** CTA using the existing contact/intake path if practical.

Do not build a dedicated intake application unless the existing path demonstrably cannot support the minimum fields.

Minimum intake data:
- person name;
- business name;
- contact route;
- short description of suspected growth/follow-up problem;
- optional website URL.

Do not ask for client databases or sensitive customer information through a public form.

## Phase 4 — organic international expansion

Before payment gateway readiness, CorpFlowAI may market internationally through public content and organic distribution.

Recommended target language:
- SMEs;
- owner-led businesses;
- professional services;
- appointment-led businesses;
- selected service companies.

Do not make Mauritius the default subject of global articles.

Use Mauritius-specific pages only when local context materially changes the advice, process, payment route, regulation or buyer problem.

## Phase 5 — paid acquisition only after readiness decision

Paid advertising is optional, not assumed.

Do not spend until:
- payment gateway/commercial collection path is confirmed for target market;
- offer and CTA have organic/qualitative validation;
- landing page + intake are verified;
- target geography is explicit;
- budget and stop-loss are approved by Anton;
- measurement is in place;
- follow-up capacity exists.

Paid ads should amplify a working commercial path, not compensate for an unclear proposition.

## Editorial cadence

Do not commit to “X blogs every week.”

Use a quality and commercial-question cadence:
- publish the initial pillar + four flagship articles;
- monitor what buyers ask;
- convert recurring sales questions into new articles;
- convert real delivery lessons into proof-rich content where client confidentiality permits;
- update cornerstone pages when the operating model changes.

## Measurement

Track first:
- pages indexed;
- impressions/search queries;
- organic visits;
- AI/search referral traffic where visible;
- article → Managed Growth clicks;
- Managed Growth → Growth Leakage Review CTA clicks;
- completed enquiries;
- qualified conversations;
- paid pilots attributable to the path.

Avoid vanity-first reporting around post counts or raw impressions without buyer movement.

## Success condition

The content programme is commercially successful when it helps create:

**discoverability → qualified conversation → diagnosis → paid managed-function pilot**

Publication volume is not the goal.
