## Atom CF-MF-0001 — Managed Business Functions

**Audience:** SME owner/operator
**Funnel stage:** Awareness / consideration
**Buyer problem:** The business needs specialist functions but cannot justify full departments; buying software does not ensure the work is performed.
**Desired outcome:** Reliable performance of a defined business function.

**Approved primary claim:**
CorpFlowAI operates enterprise-grade business functions for SMEs using the appropriate mix of people, AI, software, automation and existing business systems.

**Mechanism:**
Define job → inputs → outputs → authority → operating procedure → systems → exceptions → evidence → service level.

**Differentiators:**
- function before tool;
- clear authority;
- human oversight where needed;
- evidence and exception handling;
- use existing systems where practical;
- technology may change without redefining the job.

**Disallowed claims:**
- replaces your whole team;
- fully autonomous business;
- guaranteed savings/revenue;
- all-in-one AI platform.

**Proof status:** Mechanism/process proof; customer outcome proof still needed.

**Objection:** “Is this just outsourcing?”

**Approved response:** It is a form of managed service, but the scope is defined around a specific business function, its authority, evidence and service level rather than simply supplying labour or software.

**Primary CTA:** Request a Growth Leakage Review
**Validation asset:** `01_WHAT_IS_A_MANAGED_BUSINESS_FUNCTION.md`

## Atom CF-MG-0001 — Managed Growth

**Audience:** SME owner/operator
**Funnel stage:** Awareness / consideration / conversion
**Buyer problem:** Marketing, lead handling, follow-up, retention and customer expansion are fragmented across channels and people.
**Desired outcome:** One managed operating chain for commercial opportunities.

**Approved primary claim:**
Managed Growth operates the processes that help a business find, attract, capture, nurture, convert, retain, expand and recover commercial opportunities.

**Approved shorthand:**
**Get customers. Develop them. Keep them. Grow them.**

**Mechanism:**
Find → Attract → Capture → Nurture → Convert → Retain → Expand → Recover.

**Disallowed claims:**
- guaranteed growth;
- guaranteed ROAS;
- every lead becomes a customer;
- CorpFlowAI replaces the sales team.

**Proof status:** Mechanism-led; real client outcome proof needed later.

**Objection:** “Is this just a marketing agency?”

**Approved response:** A marketing agency may manage channels or campaigns. Managed Growth follows the operating chain beyond attention into enquiry handling, follow-up, retention, expansion and recovery.

**Primary CTA:** Request a Growth Leakage Review
**Validation asset:** `MANAGED_GROWTH_LANDING_PAGE_V1.md`

## Atom CF-MG-0002 — Lead handling

**Audience:** SME owner/operator already generating enquiries
**Funnel stage:** Awareness / consideration
**Buyer problem:** Leads are created but the handoff between enquiry and next action is unreliable.
**Desired outcome:** Qualifying enquiries are visible, owned, followed up and escalated.

**Approved primary claim:**
Lead generation is not enough. The business needs an operating process that owns what happens after the enquiry arrives.

**Mechanism:**
Enquiry → Capture → Qualification → Response → Next action → Follow-up → Outcome → Evidence.

**Disallowed claims:**
- never miss a lead again;
- instant response guarantees sales;
- chatbot equals sales process.

**Proof status:** Process-led; no conversion uplift claim.

**Objection:** “Can't a CRM do this?”

**Approved response:** A CRM can hold the record. The managed function defines who or what performs the next action, within what service level, with what escalation and evidence.

**Primary CTA:** Request a Growth Leakage Review
**Validation asset:** `03_LEAD_GENERATION_IS_NOT_ENOUGH.md`

## Atom CF-MG-0003 — Revenue leakage

**Audience:** SME owner/operator
**Funnel stage:** Awareness / consideration / conversion
**Buyer problem:** Missed enquiries, stale quotes, cancellations, dormant prospects and inactive customers contain unresolved commercial work.
**Desired outcome:** Identify and manage the highest-value credible leak before buying more leads/software.

**Approved primary claim:**
Some growth opportunities are already inside the business; the first job is to identify which unresolved opportunities need action and ownership.

**Mechanism:**
Map commercial chain → identify unresolved work → validate evidence → prioritize by value/ease/risk → operate one recovery function.

**Disallowed claims:**
- all unresolved items equal revenue;
- guaranteed recovered revenue;
- fabricated percentage leakage.

**Proof status:** Diagnostic; any numbers are illustrative unless client evidence is approved.

**Objection:** “How much money am I losing?”

**Approved response:** Do not guess. First identify unresolved commercial work and connect it to real quotes/bookings/transactions where evidence supports attribution.

**Primary CTA:** Request a Growth Leakage Review
**Validation asset:** `04_REVENUE_LOST_THROUGH_POOR_FOLLOW_UP.md`

# Initial social reuse set

These are draft attention assets. Each must link to its companion validation asset when published.

## Social 1 — Managed business function

**Hook:** Buying software is not the same as getting the job done.

A CRM can exist while leads still go stale.

Accounting software can exist while debtors remain untouched.

A marketing platform can exist while nobody publishes or follows up consistently.

The capability is there. The function still is not being performed reliably.

That is the idea behind a **managed business function**: define the job, authority, exceptions, evidence and service level — then operate it.

CorpFlowAI's proposition is simple:

**Hire the function, not the software.**

**CTA:** Read: What Is a Managed Business Function?

**Validation asset:** Article 1.

## Social 2 — Managed Growth

**Hook:** “We generated 47 leads” is not the end of the marketing report.

The next questions are more useful:

How many were contacted?
How many were qualified?
How many progressed?
How many are still waiting for action?

That is why we are framing marketing as **Managed Growth** rather than just marketing automation.

Find → Attract → Capture → Nurture → Convert → Retain → Expand → Recover.

**CTA:** See how Managed Growth works.

**Validation asset:** Managed Growth pillar/article.

## Social 3 — Don't buy more leads yet

**Hook:** Before buying more leads, check what happened to the leads you already paid to create.

Look for:
- unanswered enquiries;
- quotes with no next action;
- cancelled appointments never rescheduled;
- dormant prospects;
- customers who quietly stopped buying.

Those are not automatically “lost revenue.”

They are unresolved commercial work.

Find the real leak first. Then decide what is worth fixing.

**CTA:** Read: How Much Revenue Are You Losing Through Poor Follow-Up?

**Validation asset:** Article 4.

## Social 4 — AI is not the proposition

**Hook:** AI capability is getting cheaper. Reliable business execution is not.

Models, automation tools and software will keep changing.

The durable value is knowing:
- what job has to happen;
- what context matters;
- what authority exists;
- what requires escalation;
- what evidence proves the job happened.

**Technology changes. Your business function keeps working.**

**CTA:** Read: What Is a Managed Business Function?

**Validation asset:** Article 1.
