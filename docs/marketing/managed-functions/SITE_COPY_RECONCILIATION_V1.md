# CorpFlowAI Managed Functions — Site Copy Reconciliation v1

**Status:** Docs-only copy migration map.
**Source issue:** #1274
**Purpose:** Align the existing CorpFlowAI public estate with the managed-business-function strategy without invalidating current revenue assets or triggering a site-wide redesign.

## 1. Migration principle

Do not replace everything.

Preserve useful existing commercial work and move it under a stronger parent category.

New hierarchy:

**CorpFlowAI**
→ **Managed Business Functions**
→ **Managed Growth**
→ specific managed functions/offers such as **Lead Rescue / Enquiry Recovery**

Website Rescue remains a separate specific commercial offer/capability where still active, but it should no longer define CorpFlowAI's full identity.

## 2. Core message changes

### Company identity

**Old/narrow direction:** practical AI-assisted workflows; capture/route/follow-up as the primary identity.

**New parent proposition:**

**Enterprise-grade business functions, operated for SMEs.**

Supporting:

**Get the business function you need, without building the department around it.**

Use:

**Hire the function, not the software.**

Do not lead with:
- AI agents;
- automation;
- dashboards;
- workflows;
- chatbot capability;
- “AI-powered everything.”

Those can appear only as implementation mechanisms beneath the business outcome.

- an AI automation vendor;
- a website/lead product shop.

Do not remove verified assets merely because the parent category has changed.

## 4. About page

### Contradiction to resolve

Current wording that says CorpFlowAI is “not a growth platform” conflicts superficially with the new **Managed Growth** family.

The intent can be preserved by clarifying the difference between a platform and a managed function.

### Recommended replacement

**CorpFlowAI is not another self-service growth platform.**

We operate defined business functions with you. The software, AI models and automation underneath may change; the agreed job, authority, evidence and service level remain clear.

Suggested expanded paragraph:

> CorpFlowAI operates managed business functions for SMEs. We are not another self-service software platform that leaves your team responsible for making the tools work. We define the job, connect the necessary systems, operate the agreed process, escalate exceptions and provide visible evidence of what happened.

### About-page company description

Replace narrow “capture, route, follow up and make visible” company definition with:

> CorpFlowAI helps SMEs access business functions they need but may not want to staff as full departments. We combine people, AI, established software, automation and client systems to operate agreed functions with clear authority, evidence and accountability.

Then retain capture/route/follow-up as examples of how some functions operate.

## 5. Services / What We Do page

### Recommended architecture

Use four high-level families:

1. **Managed Growth** — find, attract, capture, nurture, convert, retain, expand and recover opportunity.
2. **Managed Finance Operations** — debtors, creditors, reconciliation preparation, payment preparation and finance reporting.
3. **Managed Business Operations** — scheduling, documents, stock/reorder, procurement administration and recurring operations.
4. **Managed Management Intelligence** — daily/weekly business pulse, exceptions, pipeline and management reporting.

### Launch-depth rule

Only Managed Growth needs a fully developed commercial page in the first implementation.

The other three families can remain concise overview cards/sections marked as areas CorpFlowAI can scope, avoiding the impression of ten simultaneously productized services.
