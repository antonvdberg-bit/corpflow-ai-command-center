## The problem

### Growth leaks between departments, channels and people

The right prospects are not identified.

Content is inconsistent.

Advertising is disconnected from follow-up.

Enquiries arrive and wait.

Quotes go quiet.

Existing customers are forgotten.

Past customers disappear.

Nobody can clearly say which opportunities still need action.

The problem is not always a lack of marketing tools.

It is often that no one owns the whole commercial operating chain.

## The managed-growth chain

### Find

Identify the people, businesses, markets and opportunities most likely to matter.

This can include target-market research, prospect identification, account research, dormant prospects and opportunity monitoring.

### Attract

Give the right prospects a reason to notice the business.

Depending on the business, this can include useful content, social media, video, search visibility, landing pages, email and paid advertising.

The channels can change. The commercial objective does not.

### Capture

Make sure interest becomes visible.

Website enquiries, WhatsApp, email, social messages, calls, forms and campaign responses should not disappear into separate inboxes or depend on somebody remembering to act.

### Nurture

Not every prospect is ready today.

Managed Growth keeps appropriate opportunities moving through useful, timely follow-up until they act, decline or enter a later follow-up cycle.

### Convert

Manage the operating work around conversion: appointment scheduling, quotation follow-up, reminders, unanswered questions, stale opportunities and handoff to the person responsible for closing the business.

CorpFlowAI does not need to replace the salesperson. We manage the work around them.

### Retain

The customer relationship should not disappear from view after the first sale.

Managed Growth can support onboarding follow-up, review requests, service reminders, renewals, satisfaction checks and appropriate re-engagement.

### Expand

Existing customers may have legitimate needs for other products, services, upgrades or renewals.

Managed Growth can identify relevant cross-sell, upsell and renewal opportunities, route them for approval where required, and track the outcome.

### Recover

Some revenue opportunities are already sitting inside the business.

Missed enquiries. Old quotations. Cancelled appointments. Dormant prospects. Lapsed customers. Unanswered messages. Overdue follow-ups.

Managed Growth helps identify those opportunities and move appropriate ones back into action.

## FAQ

### Is Managed Growth the same as a marketing agency?

No. A marketing agency may manage channels or campaigns. Managed Growth follows the commercial chain beyond attention and lead generation into capture, nurturing, follow-up, retention, expansion and recovery.

### Is this marketing automation?

Automation may be used, but it is not the service. CorpFlowAI operates the function with rules, authority, exceptions, evidence and human oversight where needed.

### Will we need to replace our CRM or website?

Usually not. The default is to use useful systems you already have and change only what the operating requirement genuinely demands.

### Do you guarantee more revenue?

No. Revenue depends on product fit, pricing, timing, demand and many factors outside the process. CorpFlowAI is accountable for competent performance of the agreed function and the agreed service level.

### Can we start with only one part?

Yes. That is the preferred starting point. Diagnose the largest credible leak and manage the smallest function that can address it.
