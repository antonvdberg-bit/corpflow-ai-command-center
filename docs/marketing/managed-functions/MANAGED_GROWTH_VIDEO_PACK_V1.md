## Video production doctrine

### Required
- first 3-second hook;
- clear on-screen text;
- one proof/mechanism moment;
- one CTA;
- companion written validation asset;
- CorpFlowAI visual standard and brand identity;
- captions/subtitles;
- transcript on the related web page where practical;
- mobile-safe framing;
- any synthetic/example numbers visibly labelled illustrative.

### Do not use
- generic robot / glowing-AI imagery;
- fake client dashboards;
- fake testimonials;
- unlabelled synthetic numbers;
- guaranteed-revenue claims;
- “fully autonomous” claims;
- stock handshake/call-centre clichés.

## Reuse plan

Each core video should yield:
- 1 main 35–50 second cut;
- 2–4 short clips/frames;
- 1 LinkedIn/Facebook post;
- 1 article embed;
- 1 static visual/carousel if worthwhile;
- 1 FAQ/objection snippet.

Do not create separate unrelated content merely to increase posting volume.

## Quality gate

- Clear in 10 seconds: Yes
- Specific buyer problem: Yes
- Proof included or flagged: Yes
- Scannable structure: Yes
- Visual direction included: Yes
- CTA singular/obvious: Yes
- Validation asset included: Yes
- No unsupported hype: Yes
- Aesthetic standard considered: Yes
