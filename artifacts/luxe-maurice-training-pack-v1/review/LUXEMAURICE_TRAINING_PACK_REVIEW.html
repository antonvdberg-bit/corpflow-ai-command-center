5. Advisor workflow summary

  1. Sign in to the LuxeMaurice tenant on lux.corpflowai.com.
  2. Open Advisor Pipeline (/client/luxe-maurice-ai/crm).
  3. Review persisted requests under Received for advisor review.
  4. Treat Demonstration records as layout examples only.
  5. Do not expect outbound messaging from this screen - contact remains human-led.

6. Operator workflow summary

  1. Open /change.
  2. Locate LEADS · LuxeMaurice CRM (concierge).
  3. Select a lead - the list focuses on that row.
  4. Use OPERATOR ACTIONS directly below the focused lead.
  5. Update stage, owner, next action, or notes where supported.
  6. Use Show all leads, Clear selection, and Focus list on this lead as needed.
