## Positioning (one sentence)

CorpFlowAI helps businesses stop losing work between messages, forms, people, and follow-ups — with **bounded, visible delivery in days**, not months of slide-deck promises.

---

## Above-the-line framing

- Sell **managed outcomes**, vertical workflows, and accountable operations.  
- AI is **leverage**, not the moat.  
- Lead Rescue is a **wedge** into managed lead/growth operations — not "our AI agent."  
- Chatbot = narrow concierge/routing aid only.

---

## Tone

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Short sentences, concrete outcomes | Revolutionary, game-changing, 10x |
| Capture, route, follow up, visibility | Guaranteed revenue, replace your team |
| Calm confidence, realistic scope | Fully autonomous, AI-powered everything |
| Human-operated where needed | Generic AI agency vibe |

## Offer messaging (canonical)

### AI Lead Rescue Sprint
- **Problem:** Enquiries slip across WhatsApp, forms, phone, DMs — follow-up is memory-based.  
- **Outcome:** Every enquiry captured, summarised, routed; owner sees a simple daily view.  
- **Price:** from **MUR 35,000** · **50% deposit** before work starts.  
- **URL:** `https://corpflowai.com/offers/ai-lead-rescue`  
- **CTA copy:** "Request discovery for Lead Rescue"

### Premium Landing Page Rescue
- **Alias (once only in metadata):** "website rescue" — always use canonical title in headlines.  
- **Problem:** Site looks dated; CTAs fragmented; buyers bounce before enquiring.  
- **Outcome:** Premium landing page with clear enquiry path and Lead Rescue-ready capture.  
- **Price:** from **MUR 45,000** · **50% deposit** before build.  
- **URL:** `https://corpflowai.com/offers/premium-landing-page-rescue`  
- **CTA copy:** "Request discovery for landing page rescue"

### Customer Recovery & Reputation Management Sprint
- **Problem:** Complaints and review backlog damage trust; recovery is ad hoc.  
- **Outcome:** Structured recovery workflow, response templates, visibility on open cases.  
- **Price:** from **MUR 45,000** · **50% deposit** before recovery work.  
- **URL:** `https://corpflowai.com/offers/customer-reputation-recovery`  
- **CTA copy:** "Request discovery for customer recovery"

### USD 150 pilot (wedge — separate)
- Warm-network AI Lead Rescue launch pilot; **not** a Mauritius sprint price.  
- May appear in flagship as optional path: `https://corpflowai.com/lead-rescue`  
- Do not conflate with MUR sprint pricing in the same breath.

## Proof language (allowed)

- "Illustrative operator view" / "synthetic demo data" on UI shots  
- "From MUR X" / "starting at" — not fixed quotes without discovery  
- "Visible output in 24–72 hours after deposit clearance" (per revenue playbook)  
- "Human review and accountability"

## Proof language (forbidden)

- Named clients without written approval  
- Revenue lift percentages  
- "Never miss a lead again" absolutes  
- Fabricated review counts or star ratings
