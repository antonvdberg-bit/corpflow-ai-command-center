# C06 — Carousel Template Spec

**Asset ID:** C06 · **Platform:** Instagram / LinkedIn carousel  
**Dimensions:** 1080 × 1080 px per slide (5–7 slides)

---

## Purpose

Educational carousel: "How CorpFlowAI sprints work" — Hook / Proof / Depth pattern.

## Slide sequence

| Slide | Title | Body |
|-------|-------|------|
| 1 | Hook | "Leads slip. Follow-ups stall. Work disappears between channels." |
| 2 | Problem | "Most agencies sell months of slides — not working output." |
| 3 | Promise | "CorpFlowAI delivers bounded sprints with visible progress in days." |
| 4 | Process | Discovery call → clear quote → 50% deposit → work starts |
| 5 | Output | "First visible output in 24–72 hours after deposit clearance." |
| 6 | Offers | Three sprint names + from-prices (MUR 35k / 45k / 45k) |
| 7 | CTA | "Request a discovery call" + corpflowai.com/contact |
