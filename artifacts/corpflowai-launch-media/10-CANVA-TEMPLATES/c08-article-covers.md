# C08 — Article Cover Template Spec

**Asset ID:** C08 · **Platform:** Blog, LinkedIn articles, newsletter header  
**Dimensions:** 1200 × 630 px (1.91:1 Open Graph) and 1920 × 1080 px (16:9)

---

## Purpose

Cover image for launch articles and LinkedIn long-form posts.

---

## Layout

| Zone | Content |
|------|---------|
| Left 55% | Article title (operator supplies per piece) |
| Right 35% | Category badge + CorpFlowAI wordmark |
| Bottom | Optional read-time pill |

---

## Visual spec

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Background | `#06111f` + optional photography with 60% dark overlay |
| Title | Inter Bold 52px `#eef6ff` max 3 lines |
| Category badge | e.g. "Lead Rescue" · fill `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.15)` · text `#2dd4bf` |
| Accent | Teal horizontal rule 4px below title |
