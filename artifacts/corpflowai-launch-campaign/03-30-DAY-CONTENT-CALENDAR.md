## Content → website flow (every post)

Every published piece must include **one** primary path:

1. **Offer-specific:** `https://corpflowai.com/offers/{slug}` (+ `#discovery` when asking for intake)
2. **General discovery:** `https://corpflowai.com/contact#discovery`
3. **Homepage offers section:** `https://corpflowai.com/#offers`

After submission, prospect receives **CF-… reference** → operator qualifies at `/admin/rapid-delivery` → ERPNext for quote/deposit/delivery.

Never link `/change`, `/admin/*`, or internal tools in public content.
