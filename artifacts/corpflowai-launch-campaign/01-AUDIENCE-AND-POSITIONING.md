# Audience and Positioning

**Status:** DRAFT ONLY · **Campaign:** CorpFlowAI 30-day commercial launch  
**Canonical offers:** `lib/public/rapid-delivery-offers.js`

---

## Primary audience — Mauritius SMBs

**Who:** Owner-operators, ops leaders, and decision-makers at small and medium businesses in Mauritius (roughly 1–50 staff).

**Sectors (priority order):**

1. **Professional services** — legal, accounting, consulting, agencies, recruitment
2. **Property and hospitality** — real estate, rentals, guesthouses, boutique hotels, tour operators
3. **Trades and home services** — contractors, renovation, solar, security, landscaping
4. **Clinics and wellness** — dental, beauty, physio, admin-heavy practices (enquiry follow-up only — not clinical advice)
5. **Retail and F&B with enquiry volume** — restaurants, catering, event suppliers with WhatsApp-heavy intake

**Shared pain (buyer language):**

- "Enquiries get lost between WhatsApp, Facebook, email, and the website"
- "We reply too late and lose the job to whoever calls back first"
- "Our site doesn't explain the offer — people bounce"
- "Bad reviews and unanswered complaints are hurting us"
- "We don't have a visible follow-up path — it's all in someone's head"

**What they want (outcomes, not tools):**

- Fewer missed enquiries
- Clearer follow-up everyone can see
- Better conversion path from first contact to booked job
- One useful workflow automated with bounded delivery
- Preview before production, controlled handover

---

## Secondary audience — international, Mauritius-contractable

**Who:** Buyers outside Mauritius who can **contract and pay for Mauritius-delivered sprints** (MUR bank transfer or agreed path).

**In scope:** Warm referrals, diaspora owners with Mauritius operations, regional partners who introduce Mauritius clients.

**Out of scope:** Generic offshore outsourcing pitch, "cheap AI labour" positioning, cold international prospecting without a warm path.

---

## Explicitly NOT our audience

| Segment | Why not |
| ------- | ------- |
| Enterprise with procurement cycles | Sprint model is 15-min discovery + 50% deposit — wrong fit for RFP cycles |
| Buyers wanting "full CRM build" or "AI agent" | Below-the-line commodity framing — refer to doctrine |
| Cold-scraped contact lists | Forbidden — warm network only |
| Regulated-data verticals without DPA | Security review gate first |
| Teams happy with current follow-up | No pain = no sprint |

## Positioning statement

**CorpFlowAI helps Mauritius businesses stop losing revenue because digital operations are slow, fragmented, or invisible.**

We deliver **bounded rapid sprints** — one accountable workflow, visible output in 24–72 hours after deposit clearance, preview before production, documented handover. Not a months-long IT project. Not generic "AI transformation."

**Above-the-line frame:** Managed outcomes, vertical workflow depth, operator accountability. AI is leverage inside the workflow — not the headline.

## Positioning pillars

| Pillar | Message | Proof type |
| ------ | ------- | ---------- |
| **Speed with substance** | First visible working output in 24–72 hours | Structural (timeline in offer pages) |
| **Bounded delivery** | Fixed sprint scope, 50% deposit, written quote | Offer page + playbook |
| **Visible follow-up** | Daily lead list / preview link / recovery plan — not slides | Validation asset (offer pages, `/standards`, `/process`) |
| **Accountability** | Named operator, handover doc, no black box | Process links on corpflowai.com |
| **Mauritius-first** | Same timezone, MUR pricing, local payment path | MUR 35k–45k sprints, ERPNext invoice |

## Discovery questions (for calls — not outreach copy)

Use on 15-min discovery calls after intake submission:

1. How do enquiries reach you today? (channels)
2. Walk me through the last enquiry that slipped — what happened?
3. Who owns follow-up when you're busy?
4. What would "success in 72 hours" look like for you?
5. Authority to approve MUR 17,500–22,500 deposit this month?
